Underwater networks, from protocol stack to modems

UnetStack is a networking stack that extends communication networks underwater. Built on an agent-based framework, it covers design, simulation, testing and deployment, from the protocol stack to a software-defined acoustic modem.

Compose agents and protocols on a media-agnostic stack.
